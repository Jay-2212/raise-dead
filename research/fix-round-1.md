Title prompt text: larger (26px), colour #f3ecc8 with dark stroke so it is readable on the key art.
2. **Wizard vs grave placement**: `teleportToGrave(id)` must put the wizard ~40 art px *below* (in front of) the stone base, never overlapping the stone. During the ritual the wizard must face up toward the grave (attack_behind anim) and stand in front of it, so the stone and its plain→cracked→rubble swap are clearly visible.
3. **Rune circle**: currently it is drawn over the wizard and too big. Centre it on the ground between wizard and grave, scale so its diameter is ~90 art px, depth just above the ground/below the wizard and stone (y-sorted), ADD blend.
4. **Plaque popup**: unreadable at present (tiny text, years/cause of death missing, moon emblem covers the name). Render it in UIScene at zoom 1 (screen pixels), ~440×210 px NineSlice of ui/frame_panel.png, anchored above the grave's screen position and clamped inside the 1280×720 view. Content inside the inner area with ~34 px padding top: medallion image 56 px (resurrectable only) on the left, then name (Pixelify Sans 20px, #d9f2ee), years "born – died" (#c9b98a 15px), epitaph (Cinzel italic 15px, word-wrapped), "Cause of death: …" (13px, #9aa8b8, word-wrapped). No text may overflow the frame.
5. **Paths**: gravel paths currently dominate the ground. Make paths ~48 art px wide (a central plaza of ~160×120 art px around spawn, plus a cross and one ring path), so grass is clearly the majority. Ensure 25–35 trees exist, several visible within one screen of spawn (edge of plaza and between grave rows), none on paths/graves. Keep footstep surface detection in sync with the new path rects.
6. **App overlay**: the panel is off-centre (left) and its border-image shows dark square corners. Centre it exactly in the viewport (`position:fixed; inset:0; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center` wrapper), make the corners transparent (no background-color outside the border-image; put the navy fill only in the inner content box), width min(92vw, 960px), height min(86vh, 600px). Fade-in/out with CSS opacity + slight translateY.
7. **After exit**: the stone must be back to `tomb_plain` (screenshot showed rubble). Also hide the plaque during the ritual and overlay.
8. **Credits screen**: currently shows raw markdown (`#`, `##`, `|` table rows) and lines overlap; the graveyard shows through. Use a fully opaque navy (#0e1526) background; parse CREDITS.md: headings → bold section titles (strip #), table rows → "Asset — Author — License — URL" lines (skip the header and `|---|` separator rows), strip `**`/backticks. Use `wordWrap: { width: 1100, useAdvancedWrap: true }` and lay out each block below the previous using its measured height so nothing overlaps. Keep the two required CC-BY attribution lines pinned at the top. Scroll with wheel/drag/arrow keys, Esc/Back returns.
9. Add a favicon (inline SVG data URI link in index.html is fine) to stop the /favicon.ico 404.
